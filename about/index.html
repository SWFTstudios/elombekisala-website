About

I’m Elombe—a designer, developer, filmmaker, and entrepreneur based near New York City. I’m interested in what happens when creativity, technology, and business are combined to turn an idea into something real.

My story

Congolese-American by background, I started building websites in 2019. What began as a way to put ideas online grew into a practice that includes design, development, photography, video, automation, and product experiments.

I’m less interested in collecting job titles and more interested in building things that work—then sharing what I learn along the way.

What I believe

Creative skills are more powerful when they’re paired with business experiments. Websites, content, and systems should help real people take real next steps—not just look finished.

I believe in documenting the process honestly: wins, stalls, and lessons included.

What I’m building now

Through SWFT Studios, I help businesses grow with conversion-focused websites, photography, video, and digital systems. Client projects like Roller Reels and Snooze Lane are part of that studio practice.

On this site, I document the broader journey of building creative businesses—and invite people to follow along.
