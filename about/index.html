About · Résumé

Hi, I’m Elombe.

I’m a designer and developer in Jersey City who studied Mobile Development at Full Sail University. I started building websites in 2019 for family and friends, and I haven’t stopped since.

Today I lead web development for Core Home, where I run Shopify storefronts, launch brand sites and build internal tools for a portfolio of consumer brands. I also founded SWFT Studios, and ship products of my own, like the iOS app Snooze Lane and Learn Lingala.

What sets my work apart is range: I can direct the look, write the code, wire up payments and automation, and shoot the video. One person, one vision, no hand-offs.
