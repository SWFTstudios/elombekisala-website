Make vs Zapier for marketing and product workflows

Automation tooling sits between marketing and development. Both Make and Zapier connect apps and move data. Zapier is faster for simple if-this-then-that chains. Make is more visual and often cheaper at scale when scenarios need branches and many steps.

Zapier

Best for quick setups, non-technical owners, and a large library of app templates. Pricing scales by task count. A strong fit when you have a few critical workflows and limited complex logic.

Make

Best for multi-step flows, conditional logic, and cost-effective operations. Scenarios can run many modules per execution. The learning curve is steeper, but teams get more control over branching and data mapping.

How to choose at work

Start with Zapier when speed and clarity matter most. Add or switch to Make when task limits, step depth, or cost become the constraint. Employers care that the system is documented, monitored, and owned, not only that it exists.
