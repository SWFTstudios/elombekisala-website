The toolkit

The tools I use, and what they do for you.

I pick tools for the business, not the résumé. Here’s each one in plain terms, with the projects where it earned its place.

FiDesign

Figma

You see and approve the site before a line of code is written, so there are no surprises at launch.

WfBuild

Webflow

A custom design your team can edit like a document, without calling a developer for every change.

Used in corehome.com, Blurred Lines Entertainment, Alpha Ministries International

PlBuild

Payload CMS

A free, open-source CMS your team edits with live preview, drafts and permissions, and that you own outright. New in my toolkit for 2026.

ShCommerce

Shopify

Checkout, inventory and payments you can trust, with a storefront that looks like your brand, not a template.

Used in Brooklyn Steel Co., Manna Hydration, TAL Hydration, Thyme & Table, FIZZ

CfBuild

Cloudflare Workers

Pages load fast everywhere and hosting costs stay close to zero, even with real back-end features.

Used in Built By Me EZ, Only at the Kiosk, Learn Lingala, SWFT Studios

D1Build

D1 · KV · R2

Accounts, orders and media storage built into the same platform, with no extra servers to rent or patch.

Used in Only at the Kiosk, Learn Lingala, Blurred Lines Entertainment

SwBuild

Swift / SwiftUI

Native iPhone apps that feel at home on iOS and can use location, notifications and subscriptions.

Used in Snooze Lane

GsMotion

GSAP · Lenis

Smooth, considered motion that guides the eye to what matters and makes a brand feel premium.

Used in FIZZ, People of Spice, Roller Reels, Winslow Pantry

3DMotion

Three.js

Products and ideas people can rotate and explore in the browser, no app required.

Used in Core Home Finish Library, Only at the Kiosk

LoMotion

Lottie

Designer-made animation that stays sharp on every screen and weighs a fraction of a video.

Used in FIZZ

StCommerce

Stripe

Take payments, deposits and subscriptions online the day you launch.

Used in Built By Me EZ, Only at the Kiosk, SWFT Studios

AtAutomation

Airtable

Every lead, client and order lands in one table you can sort, filter and share.

Used in Built By Me EZ, People of Spice, SWFT Studios

CaAutomation

Cal.com

Clients book themselves into your calendar, so you stop going back and forth.

Used in Built By Me EZ

FbBuild

Firebase

Sign-in, sync and cloud functions for apps, ready to scale with your users.

Used in Snooze Lane

TwAutomation

Twilio

Texts and calls that reach customers when an email will not.

Used in Snooze Lane, Learn Lingala

AiAI

Claude API

AI features that do real work, like drafting, tutoring and summarising, with guardrails on cost and accuracy.

Used in Learn Lingala, SWFT Studios

Make · Zapier

How we’d work together

A clear process, no black box.

You see progress every week, and you own everything at the end: code, accounts, docs.

  1. 01

    Discover

    A call to understand the business, the customer and what success looks like. You get a written scope and plan.

  2. 02

    Design

    Direction, then screens in Figma. You review real layouts with real copy, and we refine together.

  3. 03

    Build

    Development with weekly previews. Everything tested on phones, tablets and desktops, and checked for speed and accessibility.

  4. 04

    Launch & grow

    Go live, connect analytics, hand over docs and training, then keep improving from the data.
