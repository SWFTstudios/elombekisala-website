process

How a project runs.

  1. 01

    Discover

    A call to understand the business, the customer and what success looks like. You get a written scope and plan.

  2. 02

    Design

    Direction, then screens in Figma. You review real layouts with real copy, and we refine together.

  3. 03

    Build

    Development with weekly previews. Everything tested on phones, tablets and desktops, and checked for speed and accessibility.

  4. 04

    Launch & grow

    Go live, connect analytics, hand over docs and training, then keep improving from the data.
