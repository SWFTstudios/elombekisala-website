Picking a CMS for teams that publish content

February 22, 2025 · 5 min read

CMS choice is a development decision with content and marketing consequences. Marketing pages and a blog often fit Webflow CMS. Product catalogs and checkout fit Shopify. Custom fronts with an API often fit a headless CMS.

Webflow CMS

Best when design and content live in one place. Strong for portfolios, blogs, team pages, and marketing sites where editors need a visual workflow without a separate admin for every change.

Shopify

Built for products and checkout. Use it when selling is the primary job. Pair with Webflow or another front end when the brand experience needs more than a theme can deliver.

Headless

Choose headless when you need maximum flexibility and have development capacity. Content lives in the CMS; the site or app fetches it via API. That model scales across channels, but it costs more to build and maintain than a visual CMS.
