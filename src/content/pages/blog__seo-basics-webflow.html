SEO and content structure that support organic growth

February 18, 2025 · 4 min read

Content and marketing teams need sites that are easy to understand for people and for search engines. On Webflow, that means unique titles and meta descriptions, one clear H1, logical heading order, clean URLs, and fast pages.

Meta and titles

Set a unique title and meta description in each page's settings. Keep titles under about 60 characters and descriptions under about 160 so snippets stay readable in search results.

Structure and performance

Use headings in order (H1, then H2, then H3). Add alt text to images. Optimize media in Webflow and avoid heavy custom scripts that slow load time. Employers look for this kind of operational SEO judgment because it compounds with every new page of content.
