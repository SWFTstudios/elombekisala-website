Choosing Shopify, Webflow, or both for growth

February 1, 2025 · 6 min read

Stack choice is a development and marketing decision. Shopify is built for products, cart, checkout, and payments. Webflow is built for custom marketing sites and content-led experiences. Using both (Webflow for brand and landing pages, Shopify for the store) is a common pattern when growth teams need both.

When Shopify alone is enough

If the primary goal is selling online quickly and theme customization is acceptable, Shopify is the default. You trade some design flexibility for speed to launch and a mature commerce toolchain.

When Webflow should lead the experience

When brand storytelling, landing pages, and editorial content matter as much as the cart, Webflow gives designers and marketers more control. Commerce can sit behind Shopify Buy Button, custom integrations, or a headless setup.

How teams decide

Map the primary conversion (purchase vs lead), who owns content updates, and how unique the front-end needs to feel. Shopify for commerce. Webflow for custom design and content. Combine them when you need both without forcing one tool to do the other's job.
