Alpha Ministries International

A Webflow marketing site for a Christ-centered ministry serving people across cultures. Clear pathways for ministries, events, leadership, and donations.

Visit alphaministriesinternational.org

The problem

Alpha Ministries needed a modern public site that could present multiple ministry areas, promote events, introduce leadership, and make it easy to donate, without relying on a hard-to-update legacy layout.

Approach

We structured the site around discovery: hero storytelling, ministry cards, events, and leadership. Content hierarchy keeps visitors oriented while donation and learn-more CTAs stay visible.

Build

Custom Webflow design with responsive layouts, CMS-ready sections for ministries and events, and clear navigation across Home, About, Ministries, Events, Contact, and Donate.

Results

A polished desktop and mobile experience that communicates the ministry's mission, highlights programs like Children's Ministry, ESL, Outreach, and South Asian Fellowship, and supports ongoing content updates.
