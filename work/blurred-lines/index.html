brand site · 2023–26

Blurred Lines Entertainment

// result

Video moved to R2, the whole site served from the edge

Blurred Lines Entertainment has hosted hundreds of high-end weddings. Yanko wanted a site that felt like his events: bold, modern and full of moments. I designed and built it in Webflow, then moved it to Cloudflare Workers with videos on R2 for speed and lower cost.
