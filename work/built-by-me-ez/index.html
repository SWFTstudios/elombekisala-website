Built By Me EZ

Omar runs a personal training business in Teaneck, NJ. His website looked good but did nothing on its own: every sale, booking and follow-up was a text message. I rebuilt it into a platform that sells training packages, lets clients book their own sessions, sells merch and keeps every customer in a CRM.

01

The challenge

The site was a Webflow marketing page. Packages were described but could not be bought. Booking happened by text. Leads who were not ready to pay were lost, and there was no single record of who had paid for what.

Omar needed the site to take money, hand out sessions and remember every customer, without a monthly bill for a booking platform or a server someone has to look after.

02

The approach

Keep the Webflow design he already liked and move it to Cloudflare Pages as a static export. Put every piece of server logic in one Cloudflare Worker: Stripe and Cal.com webhooks, session credits in KV, CRM writes to Airtable and email notifications.

Use a pay-first model. A client pays through Stripe, receives a private booking link, and books sessions on Cal.com until their credits run out. Clients who are not ready can save their ideal training dates instead, which lands them in the CRM as a lead.

03

What I built

01

Six package pages with checkout

1:1 and semi-private packages of 8, 12 and 16 sessions, each with its own Stripe Payment Link and Cal.com event.

02

Token-gated booking portal

After payment the Worker issues a token and stores the session count in KV. The portal embeds Cal.com and every booking webhook decrements the balance.

03

Two-tier lead funnel

“Pay now” or “Save my ideal dates”. Both paths upsert the same Clients record so nothing falls through.

04

Merch storefront

One product page for the logo tee: 3 colorways × 5 sizes at $35, Stripe checkout with shipping to 28 countries and webhook fulfillment.

05

Airtable CRM

Leads, Clients, Package Purchases, Session Bookings and Merch Orders, written automatically by the Worker.

06

A 10-chapter project book

Architecture, operations runbook and roadmap written for both developers and the owner.

Stack & why

  • WebflowThe owner keeps a design he can recognise and request changes to.
  • Cloudflare Pages + Workers + KVStatic speed with real server logic, near-zero hosting cost.
  • Stripe Payment LinksSecure checkout without building a cart.
  • Cal.comScheduling that clients already understand, embedded on-brand.
  • AirtableA CRM the owner can open and edit like a spreadsheet.
  • Google StitchFast visual exploration for the merch page, rebuilt as hand-written CSS.
