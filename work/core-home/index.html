The challenge

California AB1200 requires disclosure of chemicals in cookware. The data covered hundreds of products and colorways, and the first version could not scale.

02

The approach

Reorganize the CMS into purpose-built collections: AB1200 Version 2, Colorways, Chemicals and Products, then build a new /ab1200 page and modal on top, keeping the old version as a draft.
