Core Home Finish Library

// result

Excel and slide specs → one 3D source of truth for 108 finishes

Core Home designs kitchen and home products for dozens of brands. Render requests used to travel as spreadsheets and slide decks, and every team pictured a different finish. I built a shared web app where anyone can pick a material, scroll through finishes, choose a graphic treatment and see it live in 3D.

01

The challenge

Specs lived in Excel and PowerPoint. Product Development and Industrial Design worked from different files, finishes were described in words, and renders came back wrong.

The tool had to be simple enough for anyone at the company and accurate to the factory’s own catalog.

02

The approach

Import the factory’s v3 spreadsheet straight into the catalog with a script, so the source of truth stays the factory’s data.

Design around one screen: material tabs, a finish wheel with search and filters, a graphic application shelf, a specs card and a real-time 3D preview.

03

What I built

01

Material tabs and finish wheel

Ceramic, Glass, Stainless Steel and Plastic, with search, sort and filters by color, style and process.

02

Live 3D preview

Three.js with orbit controls and a light/dark theme so finishes read correctly.

03

Graphic application shelf

Water decal, gloss 3D print, silkscreen, heat transfer, laser etch.

04

Specs card

Durability, cost and property notes for every finish, plus “Add to folder”.

05

Spreadsheet importer

Node + xlsx turns the factory workbook into the catalog JSON and D1 seed.

06

Project book

13 chapters, from “why we built this” for the C-suite to deployment for developers.
