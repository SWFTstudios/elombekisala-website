FIZZ

A Webflow prototype → 16 motion modules and a matching Shopify theme

FIZZ makes a bottle that carbonates water anywhere. No cans, no countertop machine. The site had to make a hardware product feel like a lifestyle. I took the Webflow prototype, rebuilt it as a fast static site with a full motion system, and built a Shopify theme to match.

The challenge

The Webflow prototype relied on IX2 interactions that were hard to maintain and slow on phones. Page changes cut the motion short, and the product story was buried below a long intro.

The brand also needed a Shopify storefront that felt like the same site.

02

The approach

Rebuild each Webflow interaction as its own small module: GSAP ScrollTrigger scrubs, Lottie frame math, Barba transitions that wait for the animation to finish, and Splide carousels.

Then retell the homepage as a three-chapter bottle story and lock it down with ESLint, Stylelint, TypeScript checks and a guard against deprecated CSS classes.

03

What I built

01

Scroll-scrubbed Lottie

A 500vh track scrubs the animation frame by frame while the layer stays pinned.

02

3D z-scroll Explore page

A camera that flies through product facts with a loader, HUD and modal details.

03

Barba + Lottie transitions

Prefetched pages, and a transition that resolves on the Lottie “complete” event.

04

Shop and six PDPs

A category carousel, a colorway grid and product pages with specs, how-it-works and care.

05

Kinetic Spark Shopify theme

Editable hero copy, glass card controls and a flavor collection with Webflow-parity scroll.

06

Analytics

Google Tag Manager on every page.
