Learn Lingala

// result

Zero to conversational: 227 phrases, offline, with AI speakers

Lingala is spoken by tens of millions of people across the Congo basin, yet good learning tools barely exist. I built Learn Lingala as an installable web app: a five-level course, science-backed review, and AI chat partners that text back in real Lingala, in English or French.

01

The challenge

Most Lingala resources are word lists. Learners heading to Kinshasa need to hold real conversations, sound local and keep practicing on a phone with patchy data.

AI could fill the conversation gap, but AI translation alone is not trustworthy for a language with little training data.

02

The approach

Write the course first and make it the source of truth: verified phrases, a hand-written lexicon that breaks every word into its parts, and a test that fails if a course phrase uses an unknown word.

Use Claude where it shines, live chat practice and memory hooks, rate-limited per user and clearly marked as AI. Everything else works offline.

03

What I built

01

Five-level path

Arrive, Get around, Daily life, Conversations, Mwana mboka. Checkpoints let learners test out of what they know.

02

Study cards that teach

Commit-before-you-flip, sure-but-wrong boosts, calibration and “don’t confuse with” look-alikes, each tied to learning research.

03

Live chat practice

Text in real time with Claude-powered Lingala speakers who correct you in your language.

04

Translate & dictionary

Instant verified course matches offline, plus an AI breakdown with word-by-word glosses.

05

Offline PWA

A service worker caches the whole course; progress syncs when the connection returns.

06

Accounts and reminders

Email auth, streaks, word of the day, and email or text reminders on a 15-minute cron.
