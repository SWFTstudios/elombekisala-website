Only at the Kiosk

// result

Magazine, radio, shop and member accounts, shipped in 14 days

KIOSK sells exclusive apparel drops wrapped in culture: films, photo stories, phonk radio and car spec sheets. The brief was to make the shop feel like a world you visit, not a catalog. I built it as an issue-based magazine on a custom design system called KIOSK Glass, with its own accounts, checkout and back office.

The challenge

Drops sell on hype, and hype needs a reason to come back between drops. The brand had product and content but no place that tied them together, and the planned Shopify store was not ready.

It had to work beautifully on phones, where most of the audience is, without the 3D and video turning it into a slow page.

02

The approach

Treat the site like a magazine issue. A dotted 3D globe pins visitors on their real town with live traveler beacons, then dives to black and opens a featured stream of films, stories and products.

Sell through Stripe Checkout until Shopify launches, with a cart, orders and stock in D1, order emails and a USPS back office, so the brand could sell from day one.

03

What I built

01

KIOSK Glass design system

Frosted header groups, a Liquid Glass tab bar with real backdrop blur, editorial serif layer, page curtains and Lenis smooth scrolling.

02

Live globe hero

A zoomable wireframe globe with real coastlines, the visitor’s beacon and glowing pins for live travelers.

03

KIOSK Radio

Phonk playlists from Spotify in a site-wide player with a live waveform and a 3D song wheel.

04

The Vault shop

3D hero carousel, clothing-rack rail with on-hanger cutouts, drop alerts by size and colour.

05

Member accounts

Email sign-in codes via Cloudflare Email Service, member cards and saved sizes.

06

Checkout and back office

Stripe Checkout, orders and inventory in D1, Airtable copy, admin page with USPS shipping.

Stack & why

  • Cloudflare Workers + D1Accounts, orders and stock without a separate backend host.
  • Stripe CheckoutStart selling immediately, swap to Shopify later.
  • Lenis + custom 3DSmooth, cinematic scrolling that still respects reduced motion.
  • Spotify embedsRadio that keeps playing as you browse.
  • PlaywrightMobile screenshot and controls audits before each release.
  • sharpGenerated, optimized product imagery from one pipeline.
