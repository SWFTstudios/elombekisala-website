People of Spice

// result

44 blends from 22 kitchens, explorable from one globe

People of Spice blends spices from kitchens around the world. I launched the first site in 2025 with Airtable-connected forms, then led its 2026 redesign: darker art direction, a first-visit intro where spice and salt form a globe, and a structured spice and recipe CMS.

01

The challenge

Spice jars look alike on a shelf. The brand’s difference is where each blend comes from and what to cook with it, and the first site could not tell that story at scale.

02

The approach

Make origin the navigation. A World of Spice globe, a Spice Rack and a recipe CMS generate every page from data, and every jar links to recipes that use it.

03

What I built

01

First-visit intro

Spice and salt pour into a globe, a 0–100 counter, then fly into the hero.

02

Meet the blends

A swipeable spice dial for 12 featured blends; open a jar to see its origin and recipes.

03

Soil to table

GSAP scroll text reveals and a pinned Grow, Source, Create section.

04

Spice + recipe CMS

Generated pages, Our Story timeline and SEO for every page, with Open Graph images.

05

Airtable forms

Every form posts through Cloudflare Pages Functions into Airtable.
