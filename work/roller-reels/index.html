Booking site · 2026

Roller Reels

Roller Reels shoots rolling and static video of cars and motorcycles. Riders were asking for quotes in DMs. I built a site that shows the work, prices it clearly and lets riders build and book a shoot in under three minutes.

01

The challenge

Quotes happened in DMs, prices changed per conversation, and riders did not know what they were booking or where to meet.

02

The approach

Publish real prices, then turn the quote into a guided booking flow built for thumbs: one decision per step, auto-advance, swipe navigation and a progress bar styled like a race.

03

What I built

01

Mobile booking flow

Seven steps with compact cards, upgrade tiles revealed after a format is chosen and a running “mission build” bar.

02

Transparent pricing

Static from $200, Rollers from $300, Full Cinematic at $999, plus add-ons.

03

Midnight Ember design system

Applied across homepage, bookings, calendar and team pages.

04

Gallery

A carousel with fixed Vimeo lightboxes and locked page scroll.

05

Meetup details

The Jersey City meetup spot documented on the FAQ and in the booking flow.

06

SEO + AI search

Meta titles, schema, sitemap and robots rules that welcome AI crawlers.
