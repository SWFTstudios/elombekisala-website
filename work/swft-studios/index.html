SWFT Studios

SWFT Studios gets local businesses in NYC and North Jersey seen, chosen and booked: websites, photo and video, ads and follow-up. The site is also the business’s operating system: services with live pricing, online payments, a client portal and an AI-assisted outreach pipeline.

01

The challenge

A studio selling growth systems has to run on one. Quotes, deposits, project updates and new-client outreach were manual.

02

The approach

Build services as data and generate pages, pricing and Stripe links from it. Add a role-aware portal for clients and an admin dashboard for us, and automate outreach without losing the human approval step.

03

What I built

01

Build / Grow / Automate services

Service pages and a mega-menu generated from one catalog, with one-time and monthly pricing.

02

Shop and checkout

Cart, 50% deposit or monthly payments for every package, $0 orders for 100%-off codes.

03

Client portal

Installable PWA with a role-aware tab bar, project progress and a desktop sidebar workspace.

04

Outreach engine

Places data pull, dedupe, email discovery, scoring, throttling, unsubscribe and Claude-drafted emails with an approval page.

05

The SWFT Method

An animated, per-industry walkthrough of how customers find, choose and return to a business.

06

Growth Guide

A free customer-journey check on the web with a printable PDF.
