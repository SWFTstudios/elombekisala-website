Brand site · 2026

Winslow Pantry

Winslow Pantry brings the easy spirit of a Saturday farmer’s market into the kitchen. I built the site from the brand guidelines in English, Spanish and French, with every word in translation files, so the team can grow it without touching templates.

01

The challenge

Launch a brand in three languages with consistent copy, strong SEO and real accessibility, quickly.

02

The approach

Keep all copy in i18n JSON and generate every page, sitemap, hreflang tag and share image from it. Make the build fail on house-style violations.

03

What I built

01

Trilingual site

Home, Collection, Our Story and Contact in three languages, with hreflang and localized share images.

02

Build checks

Fails on em dashes, titles over 60 characters or descriptions over 160; lists missing translations.

03

Brand motion

A plaid page curtain, a hero that writes itself in, and ambient sway that settles within five seconds.

04

Accessibility

Color never carries meaning alone, visible focus, forced-colors and reduced-motion support.

05

Gated style guide

Basic Auth on /styleguide with a color-vision preview.

06

Launch-lineup switch

Flip the whole site between the full collection and launch products.
